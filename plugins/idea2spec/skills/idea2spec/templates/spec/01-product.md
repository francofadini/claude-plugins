> **Example (replace):**
> **Problem:** People who want a cut at their neighborhood barber call or walk in and wait; the barber loses time on the phone.
> **Promise:** "I see when there is a free chair and it is booked in two taps."
> **Principle 1. The app proposes, you choose.** The next free slot comes preselected; changing it is one tap.
> **In:** see free slots and book (F2) · reminder an hour before (F3) · cancel with Undo (F4).
> **Out:** online payment · reason: the barber gets paid in cash · revisit after 3 months of use.
> **Metric:** bookings per week made in the app · count of `appointment_created` · ≥ 30 by week 4.
